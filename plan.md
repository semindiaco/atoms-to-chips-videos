# Atoms to Chips — 60-Day Content Plan (Season 1)

Channel: Atoms to Chips (@AtomsToChips) · English · AI voice · 6–10 min videos · one new video every morning (IST)
Audience: students, engineers and curious viewers, especially in India; no prior knowledge assumed
Approach: basics → advanced, grouped into playlists, with standalone videos between them.
Rules: explain general, well-established science and industry practice only. Never reveal Debye Labs' own chamber technology or IP. Check any current facts (company plans, fab projects, numbers) with a web search on the day and keep them dated.
